PRIORE

Kidney reassessment prioritisation

PRIORE estimates the likelihood of sustained kidney function decline over the next 3 years using information already available in the primary care record. It does not require a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) result.

Open the PRIORE calculator

Opens the PRIORE calculator hosted at spark-kidney-decline.streamlit.app

Intended use

PRIORE is intended to support prioritisation of kidney assessment in primary care. It does not diagnose CKD and does not replace urine ACR testing or KFRE when these are indicated.

PRIORE was developed and tested using past records. It has not been evaluated in a prospective study.

Do not enter identifiable patient information.

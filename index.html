SPARK

Stratified Primary-care Assessment of Risk for Kidney Decline

SPARK is a research-use calculator that estimates the predicted 3-year risk of sustained 30% or greater eGFR decline using routinely available primary care information without requiring albuminuria data.

Launch SPARK Calculator

Opens the SPARK application hosted at spark-kidney-decline.streamlit.app

Research-use notice

Research use only. SPARK is not a diagnostic, treatment, referral, or deployment-ready clinical decision-support tool and should not replace clinical judgement, albuminuria testing, or Kidney Failure Risk Equation assessment.

Do not enter identifiable patient information.

RIDE

Risk Index for Decline in eGFR

RIDE estimates the 3-year risk of sustained eGFR decline of 30% or greater. It uses routinely available primary care data and does not require albuminuria measurements.

RIDE is intended to help prioritise repeat eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) testing.

Input requirements

All inputs are required.

  • Age in whole years (18 to 100) and sex (female, male or unknown).
  • Current eGFR (30 to 120 mL/min/1.73 m²) and its date. Enter results reported as >90 as 90.
  • Previous eGFR results from the 2 years (730 days) before the current eGFR, each with its date. Results from the same day are averaged, and daily values below 30 or above 120 mL/min/1.73 m² are not used. At least 2 days with usable results are needed.
  • Medication records from the previous year: ACE inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker, diuretic, and 5 or more different medicines (yes or no).

If an input is missing or an entry is invalid, no estimate is given and the calculator lists what to check. If age or the current eGFR is outside these ranges, or fewer than 2 days of previous results can be used, no estimate is given and the reason is shown. Missing values are not imputed.

Intended use

RIDE supports prioritisation of kidney assessment in primary care. It does not diagnose CKD and does not replace urine ACR testing or the Kidney Failure Risk Equation (KFRE) when these are indicated.

RIDE was developed and validated using past records and has not been evaluated prospectively.

Do not enter identifiable patient information.
